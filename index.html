Your Daily Rhythm

Eating Window & Fasting Window

A simple structure to build your day around — nourishment during the day, rest for your digestive system overnight.

Eating Window

9:00 AM – 9:00 PM

Your nourishment period. Meals are structured within this window so your body gets consistent fuel while keeping a clear daily rhythm.

Fasting Window

9:00 PM – 9:00 AM

Your rest period. Digestion slows, food intake pauses, and your body has uninterrupted time to recover before the next eating window begins.
